About Zhahla

Hello, I'm Zhahla.

These are the recipes I cook for the people I love, and for myself on the evenings I need looking after. Some came from family tables, some from a tiny student kitchen at uni, and all of them are meant to be made on an ordinary day.

The name comes from what we say in Persian when someone sits down to eat: noosh-e jan, may it nourish your soul. That's what I hope each of these dishes does for you.
